Overview
The team from AWS's Generative AI Innovation Center discussed their approach to fine-tuning language models, focusing on balancing accuracy, cost, and latency. They highlighted the shift towards smaller, cheaper models under 7 billion parameters, which offer cost savings but have limitations in generalization. Their solution involves a multi-agent system that automates data augmentation and fine-tuning, using pattern analysis and quality agents to improve model performance. Experiments showed an average 29% improvement over baseline fine-tuning, with the system achieving significant cost and time savings. The team emphasized the importance of automated processes in scaling model customization.
