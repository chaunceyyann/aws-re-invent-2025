AI workloads have led to an unprecedented increase in demand, and AWS is investing in innovations to support these new technologies.


Investments in AI and Lowering Costs


•
Speaker 1 discusses the high costs of building and running AI models, with significant investments in data centers and power capacity.

Nitro System and Custom Silicon


•
Speaker 1 provides an example of AWS's deep investments in custom silicon, specifically the Nitro system.


•
The Nitro system moves virtualization off the server and onto dedicated hardware, eliminating jitter and improving performance.


•
The Nitro system also improves security, delivers higher performance, and supports a wide range of instance types.


•
Speaker 1 shares a textbook from their undergraduate degree, now updated to include the Nitro system and Graviton processors.

Introduction of Graviton Processors


•
Speaker 3 discusses the Graviton processor, designed specifically for cloud workloads, not adapted or repurposed.


•
Graviton provides better performance at lower cost, with significant benefits for various industries and organizations.


•
The Graviton processor includes custom silicon and optimizations across the full stack, including cooling.


•
Each generation of Graviton expands the set of workloads that can be served, with continuous improvements in performance.


•
Graviton 5 introduces 192 cores in a single package, providing over five times the L3 cache of previous generations.


Apple's Use of Swift and AWS

Speaker 4 from Apple discusses the use of Swift for server-side development, providing performance, safety, and ease of development.


•
Apple uses Swift to power services like App Store, Apple Music, and Apple TV, with significant performance and cost benefits.


•

Apple's spam protection feature in iOS uses Swift and Graviton to scale compute-heavy operations while maintaining privacy.


Lambda Managed Instances and Serverless Evolution


•
Speaker 3 introduces Lambda managed instances, bridging the gap between serverless simplicity and infrastructure control.


•
Lambda managed instances allow developers to choose the instance type and hardware, with AWS managing provisioning, caching, availability, and scaling.


•
The evolution of Lambda from a simple serverless model to a more flexible and capable platform is discussed.


•
Lambda managed instances enable workloads that historically leaned outside Lambda, such as video processing and high-throughput analytics.


•
The focus is on removing the operational weight of managing infrastructure, allowing developers to focus on their code and business logic.


Bedrock Service and Inference Engine


•
Speaker 3 discusses the challenges of inference workloads and the need for a new architecture, leading to the development of Bedrock.


•
Bedrock includes service tiers for different urgency levels, ensuring consistent low latency and optimized performance.


•
The architecture includes queue isolation, adaptive capacity sharing, and a durable record of work, enabling advanced scheduling strategies.


•
Bedrock integrates confidential computing to protect model weights and customer data during inference.


•
The platform supports real-time data invocation, open AI responses, and integration with AWS services like IAM and CloudWatch.
