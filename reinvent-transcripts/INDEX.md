### [AI Security Strategies with Okta's Auth0](./AI%20Security%20Strategies%20with%20Okta's%20Auth0/ai-security-strategies-with-okta's-auth0.md)

### [AWS Cloud Formation Solutions Overview](./AWS%20Cloud%20Formation%20Solutions%20Overview/aws-cloud-formation-solutions-overview.md)

### [AWS Infrastructure Innovations](./AWS%20Infrastructure%20Innovations/aws-infrastructure-innovations.md)
**Date:** December 8, 2025  
AWS's core attributes and investments in custom silicon including Nitro system and Graviton processors. Introduction of Graviton 5 with 192 cores. Lambda Managed Instances, S3 vectors for vector search, and Trainium 3 for AI workloads with 40% cost reduction.

AWS Generative AI Innovation Center's approach to fine-tuning language models. Multi-agent system for automated data augmentation and fine-tuning. Focus on smaller models under 7B parameters. Achieved 29% improvement over baseline with significant cost savings.
