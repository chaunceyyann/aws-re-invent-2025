# AWS re:Invent 2025 Overview

## Conference Details
- **Event Name**: AWS re:Invent 2025
- **Organizer**: Amazon Web Services (AWS)
- **Description**: AWS re:Invent is the premier annual cloud computing conference hosted by AWS. It brings together developers, architects, engineers, and business leaders for keynotes, training sessions, workshops, and networking. The event focuses on the latest AWS innovations, best practices, and future roadmaps across cloud technologies.
- **Official Website**: [reinvent.awsevents.com](https://reinvent.awsevents.com/)

## Time and Duration
- **Dates**: November 30 - December 4, 2025 (based on typical scheduling; confirm with official sources as dates can shift)
- **Duration**: 5 days, including pre-event activities on November 29 and post-event wrap-ups.
- **Time Zone**: Pacific Standard Time (PST), with sessions typically running from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM daily, plus evening events.

## Location
- **City**: Las Vegas, Nevada, USA
- **Venues**:
  - Primary: The Venetian Resort (main expo hall, keynotes, and sessions)
  - Additional: Mandalay Bay, MGM Grand, Wynn, and Encore (for breakout sessions, workshops, and partner events)
- **Why Las Vegas?**: Chosen for its large-scale convention facilities, accessibility, and entertainment options to facilitate networking.
- **Attendance Options**: In-person (with health and safety protocols) and virtual streaming for select sessions.

## Key Highlights
- **Expected Attendance**: Over 60,000 in-person attendees (based on previous years), plus virtual participants.
- **Format**: Mix of keynotes (e.g., from AWS CEO and leaders), hands-on labs, builder sessions, chalk talks, and an expo with 400+ partners.
- **Themes**: Emphasis on AI/ML integration, sustainable computing, edge computing, and enterprise cloud transformation.
- **Cost**: Registration starts at $1,799 for full conference pass (early bird discounts available); virtual access is free for keynotes.

For the latest updates, check the official AWS re:Invent website or AWS announcements.
